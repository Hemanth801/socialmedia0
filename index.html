Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered regarding turnaround times, confidential files, and custom formats.

Can you convert scanned or tilted PDF documents?

Yes! I utilize enterprise-grade OCR software (ABBYY FineReader and Adobe Acrobat Pro) paired with manual character auditing to ensure even tilted, low-resolution, or shaded scans are accurately extracted and aligned.

How do you guarantee 100% accuracy on financial statements?

Every converted spreadsheet undergoes an automated reconciliation audit. Debit and credit columns are summed and cross-matched with the closing statement balance on the source PDF. If the numbers don't match to the exact rupee, it is manually re-checked before delivery.

Is my sensitive financial data safe and confidential?

Absolutely. With my background in Cyber Security from Visakha Institute of Engineering & Technology and training at the Cyber Crime Police Station Lab, I adhere to strict data privacy principles. Your documents are stored on an encrypted drive during processing and are permanently deleted after delivery.

What if my file has 20,000+ rows or hundreds of pages?

Large volume files are no problem. I use Python (Pandas) and Power Query ETL scripts to batch-process large files efficiently without crashing standard Excel limits. Feel free to reach out via WhatsApp for a custom bulk quote!

What if I need adjustments after delivery?

I provide unlimited revisions until you are completely satisfied with the formatting, formulas, and organization of your final Excel sheet.
